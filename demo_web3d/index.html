Sheaf-GBP multi-agent mapping in 3D

Eight agents scan a furnished single-storey house (HouseExpo plan, 128 × 128 × 32 voxels). Each agent compresses what it saw into 1,024 Gaussian means and precisions, 2,048 bytes per message, and passes it along a radio tree. After each round of Sheaf-GBP fusion every agent decodes the whole storey from its fused posterior. Model: the current best run (batch 3S-X, seed 342101) with the loss-matched decision rule.
