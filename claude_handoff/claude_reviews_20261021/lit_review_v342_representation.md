## 0. Summary (10 lines)

1. "Owned block variable with abstention (q_k = 0 when unobserved)" is the textbook information-form fusion rule: zero information for unobserved states (Davison & Ortiz 2019 §3.2/3.4; decentralised information filter; occupancy log-odds; Curless–Levoy W(x)=0 outside the observed band). The construction is principled only when every observer's scalar is a measurement of the SAME block quantity. In v342 it is not: mu_k = tanh(MLP(mean+max-pooled CNN features)) of a partial 16x16 view (train_v342_owned.py:106-107,121-122), and in VALIDATION 79 % of an agent's own wall cells lie in blocks also encoded by a reached peer whose view overlaps its own by only Jaccard 0.21 (block_exposure.json). Averaging non-additive descriptors of different partial views is exactly the case the literature says is not composition (RoutedFusion; Wagstaff et al. 2019 latent-dimension bound; DI-Fusion/CNP make averaging exact by construction with mean-pooled encoders and count weights). So the residual own-F1 drop (.910 -> .837) is predicted qualitatively and is predicted to be concentrated in multi-observer blocks (within-shared-block drop ~9 pp if unshared blocks lose nothing).
2. The source-anchored readout is principled: belief = own factor x cavity, and from [own (mu,q), posterior (mu,Q)] the decoder can recover the cavity (peer-only) message in closed form (Davison & Ortiz eqs. 37-38); it is also the Gaussian product-of-experts decoder conditioned on one expert (Wu & Goodman 2018). But it does let own content be rendered from the own planes, so own-F1 and union-F1 cannot by themselves certify that the POSTERIOR is a readable union. Falsification test: posterior-only readout at evaluation (own planes replaced by the unobserved value) with gates on own@R8 and peer@R8 (§2.3).
3. DT head + "DT<0.5": the L1-optimal field under positional uncertainty is the per-pixel MEDIAN of the DT; for a 1-px line the valley at the true pixel is 0 only if P(perpendicular offset = 0) > 1/2. CPU simulation on real v293 maps: pixel-exact wall F1 under a discrete-Gaussian shift of sigma px goes 1.00 -> .87 -> .00 at sigma = 0.5 / 0.75 / 0.9 px at H128 (K0; H256: .87 at 0.75 px, .00 at 1.0 px): a hard cliff at ~0.9 px, in pixels, independent of H. A signed truncated field with sign-based decision stays at .95 (sigma 1.0) and .91-.93 (sigma 1.25-1.5); NMS/ridge detection is the wrong remedy here (it thins the 2-px slabs the metric scores pixel-exactly: F1 .90 even at sigma 0). With the root's measured ~8 cm localization uncertainty (encoder_information.md §2.3) this cliff sits at 128 px/20 m, i.e. right at 256 and far past at 512.
4. Single next change (Q4): replace the unsigned truncated DT by a Curless–Levoy signed truncated distance ramp (free: +min(DT_wall,T), wall: -min(DT_free,T); L1; wall := known & s<0; T in metres) in the same 64-variable, diagonal pipeline. It keeps the DT head's distance-aware gradient, removes the valley-threshold cliff, and is the same representation whose zero-crossing extraction is robust to blur in TSDF fusion. It attacks the larger of the two remaining gaps (K0 .910 rendering fidelity) but not the composition gap (-7.3 pp); .95 at R8 most likely needs both this and an additive (mean-pooled, count-precision) block code. Gates in §4.4.

## 1. Q1 — Is "owned block code with abstention" known, and does the literature predict the own-F1 drop?

### 1.1 What v342 actually does (code)
- Variable k is public block k of an 8x8 grid; the encoder is strictly block-local (PatchTrunk unfolds 16x16 patches, train_v342_owned.py:103-107) and pools with mean AND max over the 2x2 feature grid (L106) before an MLP and tanh (L121-122). q_k in [1e-4, 4] from a sigmoid (L123); abstention is the hard indicator "block has >= 1 own-known cell" (L124) multiplied into the evidence precision in the rollout (L218-219). The GBP posterior is the diagonal precision-weighted average (forest.scalar_belief, L225), re-quantised to hard8 (L226).
- The 64 wire variables therefore carry one 8-bit scalar + one precision per 2.5 m x 2.5 m block.

### 1.2 Literature placement
- Information-form fusion with zero information for unobserved states is the canonical construction. Davison & Ortiz 2019 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14139), §3.2: "The information form is preferred ... as it can represent rank deficient Gaussian distributions with zero information"; §3.4 eqs. 37-38: a variable's belief/outgoing message is the SUM of incoming (eta, Lambda). The owned arm's "q_k = 0 unless observed" is exactly a factor with zero information on that block. Same rule in occupancy-grid log-odds fusion (Elfes 1989, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/30720; Thrun 2003, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1025584807625), in multi-robot occupancy-grid merging (Birk & Carpin 2006, https://robotics.ucmerced.edu/sites/robotics.ucmerced.edu/files/page/documents/pro2006.pdf), and in volumetric range fusion: Curless & Levoy 1996 (https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/volrange/paper_1_level/paper.html) accumulate D(x) = sum w_i d_i / sum w_i with weight W(x) = sum w_i and "truncate the distance ramps and weights to the vicinity of the range points", i.e. w = 0 where a view says nothing; KinectFusion (Newcombe et al. 2011, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6162880) is the same rule with a weight cap. The v342 posterior-variance channel 1/(sum q + prior) is the analogue of W(x) and is read by the decoder as the reached-support map (disclosed in mapping_owned_pair_v342.json:81).
- Sheaf reading: block ownership with abstention is a cellular sheaf whose restriction maps are coordinate selections; tree GBP computes the precision-weighted consensus, i.e. the projection onto global sections for selection/isometric restriction maps (Hansen & Ghrist 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01513; Hansen & Gebhart 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06333; Bodnar et al. 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.04579). This matches the energy reviewer's "diagonal closure" result (only positive diagonal scalings, signed permutations and partial selections keep messages diagonal).
- Learned analogues that make AVERAGING EXACT by construction: Conditional Neural Processes aggregate per-observation encodings by a commutative mean so that a context set's representation is the mean of its parts (Garnelo et al. 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01613); Deep Sets (Zaheer et al. 2017, https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06114) rho(sum phi(x_i)); DI-Fusion (Huang et al. 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05551) encodes each local voxel as the MEAN of per-point FC features (eq. 2) and fuses frames by the count-weighted running average l <- (l w + l_t w_t)/(w + w_t), w = number of points (eq. 8); with disjoint observations this is identically the encoding of the union. Their ablation: the max-aggregation alternative is consistently worse (Tab. 1-2, Fig. 10).

### 1.3 What the literature says about averaging partially observed block descriptors
- Averaging is the optimal fusion only when each input is an unbiased measurement of the same latent scalar (RoutedFusion, Weder et al. 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04388, §1: "The average is only the optimal estimate for zero-mean Gaussian noise"; linear TSDF updates thicken thin structures and "when depth measurements of a thin object are made from opposite directions ... the surface vanishes since the linear TSDF updates cancel each other out"). Two agents seeing different sub-regions of a block produce codes of DIFFERENT patterns, not noisy measurements of one pattern; their precision-weighted mean is a code of neither pattern nor of the union unless the code is additive in the observation.
- Capacity bound for additive codes: continuous sum-decomposable set functions need latent dimension >= maximum set size (Wagstaff et al. 2019, ICML, https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09006). A block holds up to 256 cells (typically 10-40 wall cells); one scalar per block cannot be an exact additive code of arbitrary block content. Hence an end-to-end trained scalar is forced to be a compromise between (a) K0 fidelity (nonlinear, high-information code) and (b) averageability (near-linear code), and composition errors appear exactly in blocks with >= 2 observers whose views differ.
- Exposure of own content to this failure mode (CPU, VALIDATION H128 idx 0-3, equal-strata means, block_exposure.json): each agent observes 9.7 of 64 blocks; 80 % of them are also observed by a peer reached within 8 forest rounds; 79 % of own WALL cells lie in such shared blocks (47 % in blocks with >= 2 peers); mean 2.2 peers per shared block; Jaccard(own-known, peer-known) inside shared blocks = 0.21 (rooms6/N8: 0.10; rooms6/N16: 0.49). I.e. the views that get averaged are mostly complementary, the worst case for a non-additive scalar.

### 1.4 Does this predict the residual drop .910 -> .837?
- Yes, qualitatively and in sign: with 79 % of own wall cells exposed, a uniform within-shared-block loss of ~9.3 pp reproduces the observed -7.3 pp if unshared blocks lose nothing. The literature predicts the drop to be (i) zero in blocks observed by the recipient alone (posterior == own code exactly, L218-226), (ii) increasing with the number of observers per block and with view dissimilarity, (iii) smaller for the owned code than for the dense code because the dense global head mixes ALL blocks (DenseHead L132-141: flatten -> Linear; ENDPOINT: dense -13.7 pp, anchor -23.2 pp). (i)-(iii) are testable on the saved panels (train_v342_owned.py:400-403 save own, mu, q, target_k*, pred_k* for idx 0 and 8): split own wall cells by observer count and report own wall F1 per class. No GPU needed.
- It does NOT predict the magnitude from first principles; the magnitude depends on how additive the learned mu_k ended up (diag 4: non-co-adapted free codes R8 .499 vs trained .848 shows training bought most of the averageability).
- Quantisation is not the mechanism (diag 1: hard8 == continuous), but note the posterior mean is re-rounded after averaging (L226); for cavity recovery (§2.1) this adds up to half a step scaled by Q/(Q - q_own).

## 2. Q2 — Source-anchored readout: principled? can it mask a fusion failure?

### 2.1 Correspondence
- Decoder input = [own hard8 mean, own log var, posterior hard8 mean, posterior log var] (OwnedDecoder L152-156; DenseDecoder L170-172; evaluate L392 concatenates feats[0] and feats[k]). In information form the posterior is own factor + cavity: Q = q_own + Q_cav, Q mu_post = q_own mu_own + Q_cav mu_cav. Both Q and q_own are available (log var planes), so the decoder can compute Q_cav = Q - q_own and mu_cav = (Q mu_post - q_own mu_own)/Q_cav: the readout is equivalent to giving the decoder (own factor, cavity message), which is precisely the BP variable-node decomposition (Davison & Ortiz eqs. 37-38) and, for Gaussian experts, the product-of-experts posterior conditioned on one expert (Wu & Goodman 2018 MVAE, https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.05335: PoE mean = sum mu_i T_i / sum T_i, T = sum T_i, identical to the diagonal GBP rule with the 1e-4/(64N) prior as an extra expert). The MVAE also trains on sub-sampled modality subsets, the analogue of the R0/1/2/4/8 stage mixture (L31, L431-433).
- So the construction is principled and it is also the reason local refinement stopped hurting R8 (root_ledger batch 1 note).

### 2.2 How it can mask a fusion failure
- Own log var tells the decoder, per block, whether the recipient observed it (log(1/(q+prior)) vs log(1/prior)); the decoder can render own content from the own planes and use the posterior only elsewhere. Then (a) own wall F1 at R8 reflects the own planes, not the fused code; (b) for the 20 % of blocks observed by exactly one agent the "fusion" is routing (posterior == that agent's code); (c) only the shared blocks test composition, and there the decoder may legitimately down-weight the posterior. The G1 failure (-7.3 pp) shows masking is incomplete, which is evidence that composition in shared blocks is lossy and the decoder cannot fully undo it.
- The quantity the protocol is about (what others can read from the wire) is NOT in the endpoint table although it is computed: peer_wall_f1, wall_recall, wall_precision are aggregated at L395 and in the stratum summaries (L404-411). Report peer_wall_f1 at R1/R8 for both arms before anything else.

### 2.3 One falsification test (evaluation only, ~1 GPU-minute or CPU with the checkpoint)
- Posterior-only readout: run `evaluate` with the own planes replaced by the unobserved value for every block (mu_own := 0, log var_own := log(1/prior)), posterior planes unchanged. Each block then looks exactly like an "unobserved-by-me block with peer content", a configuration the decoder was trained on, so the probe is in-distribution at the block level.
- Gates (VALIDATION, equal strata, best.pt): let D_own = own_wall_F1@R8(anchored .837) - own_wall_F1@R8(posterior-only). D_own <= 3 pp: the posterior alone is a readable union and anchoring is cosmetic (H2 composition confirmed). D_own >= 10 pp, or posterior-only own@R8 below the dense arm's .722: the fused scalar in shared blocks is not readable without the own anchor -> the headline is carried by routing + anchoring, and the composition claim must be withdrawn or restricted to single-observer blocks. Also report peer_wall_F1@R8 under both readouts; it must be unchanged (it does not depend on own planes except through decoder context); a change > 2 pp means the decoder uses the own planes to localise peer content, which is a second masking channel.
- Zero-cost companion: the observer-count split of §1.4 on the saved panels.

## 3. Q3 — DT head + DT<0.5: known failure mode at 256/512, standard remedy

### 3.1 Mechanism
- Loss: L1 between softplus(wall channel) and the truncated DT (T = 4 px) on known cells, near-wall weight 4 for DT <= 1.5 px (L252-259; --dt-trunc L283). Decision: wall := softplus(.) < 0.5 inside predicted known (L262-266).
- The L1-optimal prediction under uncertainty is the per-pixel median of the target DT. For a 1-px line whose perpendicular position is uncertain, the DT at the true pixel is 0 with probability p0 = P(offset = 0) and >= 1 otherwise; the median is < 0.5 only if p0 > 1/2. This is a valley-depth test, and it fails in one step (a cliff), exactly like BCE + threshold 0 (H1), only with a distance-aware gradient around it. (L2 is worse: the mean DT at the true pixel is 1 - p0 >= 0.5 already for p0 <= 1/2.)
- The thin-structure/TSDF literature names the same two failure classes: valley/thickness interference between nearby faces (Curless & Levoy: weights must taper "to prevent surfaces on opposite sides of the object from interfering", and thin surfaces remain a stated limitation; RoutedFusion §1 items 2-3: thickening on thin structures, cancellation when observed from both sides). Here walls are 0.3 m slabs observed from both sides, i.e. two 1-px first-hit lines 2 px apart at 128, 4 px at 256, 8 px at 512 (BRIEF F3); the unsigned DT between the two faces is 1 at 128, so any blur merges the two valleys.
- Resolution scaling: T and the 1.5-px near band are fixed in pixels (L257, L283), i.e. 0.63 m / 0.23 m at 128 but 0.16 m / 0.06 m at 512, smaller than the slab thickness and than the ~8 cm localization uncertainty the encoder review measured (encoder_information.md §2.3: constant predicted wall-pixel count 86/99/106 at 128/256/512, recall 0.78/0.45/0.27). At 512 the "distance-aware" band collapses to a 1-px threshold regime; plus far-range coverage is dotted (beam spacing 0.12 m > 0.039 m cell), so the DT target itself is a chain of cones and the L1 head is pushed to "no wall" between dots.

Readings: (i) the v342 rule has a cliff at P(offset = 0) = 1/2, sigma ~0.9 px in this model, independent of H; the R8 column at 128 survives partially only because adjacent 2-px slabs keep one face at DT 0, which disappears at 256 where the faces are 4 px apart. (ii) The known-only signed rule needs p0 > P(offset toward the occluded side) = (1 - p0)/2, i.e. p0 > 1/3 (cliff ~1.2 px); supervising the field negative behind the wall removes the cliff for any symmetric error (limited by corners and slab interference: .91 at 1.5 px, .90 at 2 px). (iii) Ridge/NMS detection is NOT a remedy for this metric: it thins the 2-px slabs that the pixel-exact F1 scores (.90 at sigma 0) and its plateau test fails once the median field flattens. Caveats: integer global shifts (relative geometry preserved), smooth sub-pixel decoder outputs would soften the cliffs; the known mask is taken exact; this is a decision-rule isolation, not a prediction of absolute F1.

### 3.3 Standard remedy
- Signed truncated distance with zero-crossing extraction is the standard: Curless & Levoy / KinectFusion extract the zero isosurface by Marching Cubes; the location of a zero crossing of a monotone ramp is invariant to symmetric blur, the depth of an unsigned valley is not. DeepSDF (Park et al. 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.05103) regresses clamped signed distance for the same reason. Sub-pixel edge localisation (Canny 1986 NMS + Devernay's sub-pixel refinement, implementation: Grompone von Gioi & Randall, IPOL 2017, https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2017/216/) only pays off under a tolerance metric; with pixel-exact scoring it reduces to the sign rule.
- Boundary-aware losses for thin structures (Kervadec et al. 2019 boundary loss, https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07032; Karimi & Salcudean 2019 Hausdorff loss, https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10030; clDice for tubular structures, Shit et al. 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.07311) all use signed/distance-weighted surrogates with a sign- or skeleton-based decision, never an unsigned valley threshold.
- Metric note: boundary-detection benchmarks score with a matching tolerance (BSDS: 0.75 % of the image diagonal, Martin, Fowlkes & Malik 2004, https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2002/hash/b5c01503041b70d41d80e3dbe31bbd8c-Abstract.html, benchmark code https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Research/Projects/CS/vision/bsds/code/Benchmark/demo.m). Keep the pixel-exact headline, but also report tolerance-1-px F1 to separate existence from localisation errors (the encoder review proposed the same, E1).
- Concrete head for this pipeline: target s = +min(DT_wall, T) on free cells, s = -min(DT_free, T) on wall cells (= -1 for 1-px lines), no loss on unknown cells (the network extrapolates negative behind the first-hit line, giving the "negative behind" variant for free); T in metres (0.6 m => 4/8/16 px) and the near-wall band in metres; L1 with the existing near-wall weight; wall := predicted known & s < 0.

## 4. Q4 — The single next change most likely to move R8 union wall F1 from .85 toward .95 at 128 (64 variables, diagonal precision kept)

### 4.1 Where the remaining 15 pp are
- K0 own rendering: .910 (local iterative encoding .931, ENDPOINT L12) — a ceiling of the decoder + decision rule given one scalar per block, since better codes add only +2 pp.
- Composition loss on own content: -7.3 pp (own .837 at R8), by §1 concentrated in the 79 % of own wall cells in shared blocks.
- R8 union .848 sits 1.7 pp under the cnn_dt auto-decoder ceiling .865 (ENDPOINT L15), i.e. at 128 the fusion semantics is no longer the binding constraint; the readout is.
- Arithmetic: .95 at R8 needs K0 >= ~.96 AND composition loss <= ~1 pp. No single change delivers both. The change below targets the first (and all of 256/512); the second needs an additive block code (§4.5).

### 4.2 Hypothesis H-R1
Replacing the unsigned truncated DT by a signed truncated distance ramp with a sign decision (§3.3, everything else frozen) raises K0 own wall F1 at 128 by >= 3 pp and R8 union wall F1 by >= 3 pp, and prevents the 256 collapse that the unsigned rule predicts.

### 4.3 Evidence / counter-evidence
- For: §3.2 cliff at ~0.9 px with the measured ~8 cm (= 0.5/1/2 px) uncertainty; the anchor's K0 recall 0.78/0.45/0.27 at 128/256/512 with precision 0.91/0.83/0.63 (encoder_information.md §2.3) is a localisation signature, not a forgetting signature; the DT head already beat BCE at the ceiling (cnn_dt .865 vs cnn_bce .805 R8), which shows the distance-aware gradient matters, and the signed ramp keeps it; same representation as TSDF fusion whose zero-crossing extraction is robust to blur.
- Against: at 128 the owned arm already reaches .910 with the unsigned rule, so P(offset = 0) is already > 1/2 for most wall pixels and the 128 gain may be small (the pixels recovered are those with p0 in (1/3, 1/2) plus merged-slab pixels); doors and far glimpses (F5; door-free recall 0.44 at K0 for the anchor) are information/prior failures that no decision rule fixes; the composition gap is untouched; the sign rule introduces a new error source at frontiers if the known head misplaces the free/unknown boundary by 1 px (a free cell adjacent to a predicted-known, negative cell is a false wall) — report unknown FPR and door-free recall as guards.

### 4.4 Falsifiable experiment (one 7200 s GPU batch, matched control)
- Arms: owned-DT (re-run, identical seed 342101, 36 epochs) vs owned-SDF (only stage_loss/predict/dt targets changed: signed ramp, T = 0.6 m, near band 0.23 m, L1, wall := known & s < 0). Same data order, lease, selection rule (best.pt by VAL R8 union wall F1), fresh panel reported not selected.
- Pass (H-R1 supported): K0 own wall F1 >= .94 AND R8 union wall F1 >= .88 AND unknown FPR <= .050 AND door-free recall not lower than owned-DT by more than 2 pp. Fail: K0 < .925 or R8 < .86 -> the decision rule is not binding at 128; go to §4.5 first. Mixed (K0 up, R8 flat) -> composition is binding; go to §4.5.
- Pre-registered 256 forecast that batch 3 (owned-DT native 256, warm-started) will test without any new run: if the decoder's uncertainty is ~8 cm, the unsigned rule puts 256 at the cliff: owned-DT K0 own wall F1 at 256 < .85 and R8 < .80. If batch 3 instead gives K0 >= .90 at 256, the localisation-cliff account is weakened and §4.5 moves to first place.
- Cheap pre-check (CPU, needs only a checkpoint): histogram of softplus(wall channel) at true wall pixels at K0; the mass in [0.5, 1.5] is the recall the sign rule would recover.

### 4.5 Second change, for the composition gap (not first, stated for completeness)
Make the block scalar additive in the observation (CNP/DI-Fusion form): mu_k = sum_{own known cells in k} phi(cell, xy) / n_k, q_k = n_k x learned per-block scale, with the GBP average then equal to the union statistic for disjoint views; Wagstaff's bound says one scalar cannot do this exactly for arbitrary content, so expect a K0 cost; the matched test is K0 drop <= 3 pp against own@R8 - K0 >= -2 pp (G1 as registered). Precision = coverage count is the Curless–Levoy / DI-Fusion weight and is the cheapest partial version.

## 5. Verdict on H1/H2/H3
- H1 (precision/localisation x threshold): supported, and sharpened: the DT head moved the threshold from a logit to a valley depth but kept the cliff (§3.2). Confidence high for the mechanism at 256/512, medium for the size of the 128 gain.
- H2 (dense averaging is not union composition; owned variables compose): supported for routing and for single-observer blocks (math tests; +14 pp over dense); for shared blocks the literature predicts, and G1 shows, residual non-composition (-7.3 pp) because the scalar is non-additive; the source-anchored readout is principled but hides part of this (§2). Confidence medium-high; the §2.3 probe decides.
- H3 (information cap at 512 / 20 rooms): not addressed by this round; 1 scalar per 2.5 m block is an architectural cap before the bit cap (one block holds a door, a corner and two faces); at 128 the R8 numbers sit at the decoder ceiling, not at a bit ceiling (diag 1: hard8 == continuous).
