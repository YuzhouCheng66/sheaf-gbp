## 摘要（中文）

1. **版本账本**：从 v299 到 v340 共 ~20 个真实 GPU 批次。v299/v304/v310/v319 这条 CNN decoder 家族是全部历史中唯一在 native128 上 K0 wall F1 ≥ 0.83、R8 union wall F1 ≥ 0.60 的家族。v321 起的所有重写（scratch sheaf v321、坐标读出 v327、矩形渲染 v329/v330/v333、typed-patch v334、周期场 v335/v336、生成空间场 v337、残差 v338/v339、幅度调制 v340）**没有一个**在 K0 或 R8 上回到 v299 水平：128 K0 最好 0.579（v340）对 0.831（v310）/0.871（v304）；R8 union wall F1 最好 0.507 对 0.626；unknown FPR 0.166 对 0.065；R1 union accuracy 从 0.877 跌到 0.607。v329/v330/v333/v334/v337 五个批次 K0 恒为 0。同时 v338–v340 的每 update 耗时约 2.3 s，比 v315 CNN 在 128/256 的 0.12–0.22 s 慢 10–19 倍。512 自 v321 起被“推迟”，再没有被评估。
2. **数据**：v293 生成器中 “wall” 的监督目标是 **first-hit 表面线（1 像素厚）**，不是 0.3 m 墙板（板厚 2/4/8 像素 @128/256/512）；实测观测墙像素 100% 与 free 相邻。own-known 覆盖率 3.6–8.4%，own-known 内墙比例 128/256/512 ≈ 7.5–12.9% / 3.6–6.5% / 1.6–3.0%。256 beams 在 512 分辨率下 5 m 处间距 3.14 格：开阔地 2–3 m 处只有 81% 的格被观测，4.5–5 m 处 43%（256：4.5–5 m 处 86%；128：100%）。同一 world 在 3 个尺寸和 2 个 N 上别名（几何+连续轨迹相同，仅位姿采样随 N 变），VALIDATION 24 个 world 已被 ≥40 个版本用于选点。VALIDATION 与 TRAIN 不存在近重复（最近 TRAIN world 参数 L∞ 距离 ≥1.19 m），但分布完全相同且极低维（6/12/20 房 = 24/48/80 个连续参数）。20 房世界的房间净高仅 1.3–1.95 m。
3. **反方**：(a) 所有“R8 比 R4 多轮带来的变化”对 77.7% 的 VALIDATION 接收者来说输入源集合完全相同（R4=R8），97.9% 的接收者 R8 已到达全部源——R8 ≈ 全局 union，不是多跳难题；(b) 阈值固定为 logit≥0（train_mapping_multiscale_v310.py:444），在 1 像素正例占 2–13% 的 BCE 下，任何“F1 提升”都必须用阈值无关指标（AUPRC/best-F1）复核；(c) wall precision 只在 target-known 内计算（metrics_v277:36-46），unknown 区域的幻觉墙不扣 precision；(d) v340 中 own wall F1 在融合后反而上升 +4.7pp，与 FPR 从 0.5% 升到 17% 同时发生——“own 保护”指标必须在匹配 FPR 下读；(e) 通信账：学习方法每条有向边每轮重发 576/608 B（R8 字节恰为 R1 的 8 倍，无抑制），而强无损 raw（zlib 稀疏三值图）在 128/N8 R8 只需学习方法的 12.7%；512 N16 整个 16-agent 场景压缩后仅 19 KB（每张图 ≈1.2 KB），因此“相对强无损 raw 100×”在当前观测稀疏度下**不可能**，只有相对未压缩 2-bit raw 才有 7–50×；(f) 24 个 VALIDATION world 是有效样本量，+2pp “实质增益”规则处于噪声层；(g) v304 控制臂在 6 房 94.7% union accuracy 的同时 door-free recall 0.8%、door-false-wall 96.2%——高 accuracy 可以与全部门洞被封同时成立，v305 证明恢复 ~1% 的门洞像素即可消除 6/12 房 82%/56% 的连通性错误。
4. **结论**：H1 的机理（precision≫recall、1 像素目标、零阈值）有直接证据（v340 P 0.75–0.82 / R 0.32–0.47；v299 2 px 容差使 20 房 peer recall 36→52%），DT 头能否“大幅提升”未被证明（置信 0.45 @128/256，0.25 @512）。H2 的“dense 平均不能组合”有 60–63% 平台的间接证据，但其“公共空间所有权”疗法在本代码库中已被 v306、v329–v334 五次尝试且全部 K0=0——疗法无正向证据（置信 0.3）。H3 在 512/20 房下对 union accuracy+FPR 基本成立（已知支持边界的熵远超 512 bit），对 wall F1 也可能成立，但主要是 256-beam 传感模型造成的点状 GT 问题，不纯是 64 维的问题（置信 0.7）。**首先要做的**是：修评价协议（新 VALIDATION 世界 8–15、阈值无关指标、按 world 配对 bootstrap、匹配 FPR）；建带家具的 v293 增强器与 HouseExpo 原生 128/256/512 管线（CPU 一天）；唯一的 7200 s GPU 批：在 v310 E12 锚上做 DT 回归头 vs BCE 头的匹配对照（两臂同样重置最后一层），门槛见 §4。

### 1.1 Patterns the ledger forces one to see

P1. **Every architecture after v321 regressed against the v299 CNN family on every headline metric and never recovered.** Best post-v321 K0 at 128 is 0.579 (v340) vs 0.831 (v310 E12) / 0.871 (v304); best post-v321 R8 union wall F1 at 128 is 0.507 vs 0.626; FPR 0.166 vs 0.065; **R1 union accuracy fell from 0.877 (v319 E0 baseline) to 0.607** (v340) because R1 peer typed accuracy is 0.38 — the new family barely reads one-hop evidence. Five consecutive batches (v329, v330, v333, v334, v337) had K0 = 0.

P2. **512 silently left the programme at v321** ("deferred; unsolved") and was never evaluated again; all "128/256 discovery" numbers since then are on a 1184/96 subset.

P3. **Each new decoder was slower, not faster**: v315 CNN 0.12/0.22 s per update at 128/256 (v320); v321 0.43 s (3 sizes); v329–v335 ≈0.6–0.7 s; v338–v340 ≈2.3 s (3088 updates in ~7000 s; 2368 updates in 5563 s minus ~190 s validation). No equivalent-speedup target was met (v320), and the quality/speed frontier moved backwards.

P4. **Gates moved every version** (E4 in v333/334, E14 in v336, E18 in v337, one matched epoch in v340) and each was defined against a weaker in-family control, not against v299/v310. The only runs ever scored against the historical protections (v310, v319, v340 "historical" rows) all failed them.

P5. **"Nonzero gradients / weight deltas / exact packet replay" is reported in every ledger** as "activity" and is never evidence of fidelity (the syntheses say so themselves). The restriction "has real gradients" (README §先读结论) but v340 measured ext_q/source_q median ≈0.0115 with p99 ≈75 — heterogeneous coupling, no causal benefit shown.

P6. **Dev-set reuse**: the same 24 VALIDATION worlds (8 indices × 3 room counts) selected the v299 r1 pair, v304, v310 anchor epoch (max R8 peer F1), v316 E32, v319, and all v321–v340 gates. `checked_spec` caps VALIDATION at index < 8 (mapping_native_bank_v306.py:52), so VALIDATION indices 8–15 have **never been drawn** — a free fresh dev panel (see §4).

### 1.2 Claims in the handoff that lack evidence or are overstated

- F1 "hard 8-bit (**127 levels**)": the hard mean is `(code*127).round()` on a tanh output, i.e. integers −127..127 = **255 levels** (mapping_belief_readout_v282.py:41-43). Bits are ≈8 as stated; "127 levels" is wrong.
- F5 "v335–v340 … **no walls at 256 at all**": v340 256 R8 wall F1 .449 with precision .82 (curves E17) — walls exist in aggregate; the single fixed panel is not population evidence. The right statement is "256 K0 wall recall .35".
- F2 anchor numbers are **VALIDATION-selected** (E12 picked as the max R8 peer F1 of the v310 curve, v318:8) and therefore optimistically biased by an unknown amount; no fresh-world number exists for any anchor.
- README §2.2 "v319 trained E8 native128 R8 wall F1 63.51%": true, but at the same epoch union accuracy fell at all sizes and 256/512 wall F1 fell; K0 own F1 fell at 128/512. Not a candidate.
- "v335 has genuine source association" (v335 synth): the correct-vs-cyclic/mean-code BCE test also "passed slightly" in v333 with zero recall (v333 ledger `source_association_caveat`); it is a weak test and should not be read as information sufficiency.
- v316's E32 512 numbers (91.89/45.29/12.74) are reported in the README as a "counter-example that no checkpoint dominates" — but no K0 for the scratch pair is recorded anywhere I was given, and its 128 R8 wall F1 (54.5) is 8 pp below v310.
- F6 "privileged fits cap at .85–.88 / .67–.75" is from **two** TRAIN rooms-20 N8 witnesses with a 400-step cap (attainability synthesis `counterevidence`); it is a lower bound on attainability, not a cap.
- F7 "12-room layout ≈36 numbers / 20 rooms ≈60": in this generator a world is exactly 4 numbers per room (y0, y1, width, door centre; `world_spec` lines 58-64) with shared corridor x = 8.9/11.1, so 6/12/20 rooms = 24/48/80 continuous parameters (my `probe_dup.py`). The layout is **not** the binding entropy term; the own/union known-support boundary is (see §3, H3).

### 2.1 Exact v293 generator semantics (read: `reference_source_v310/scripts/mapping_multiroom_data_v293.py`, `prepare_houseexpo_pilot_v121.py`, `mapping_native_bank_v306.py`)

- **World** (`world_spec`, v293:47-69): seed = sha256(`v293:{split}:{index}:corridor-spine:{rooms}`), so the world is identical for all sizes and all N. Corridor x∈[9.2,10.8] (1.6 m), y∈[0.6,19.4]. Per side `rooms/2` stacked rooms; heights 1.4 + (18.8−1.4·count)·share, clearances 0.15 m each side ⇒ **0.3 m slab between vertically adjacent rooms**; room x-extent [8.9−w, 8.9] / [11.1, 11.1+w], w∈U(4.4,8.1) ⇒ **0.3 m slab between room and corridor**; door rectangle [8.9, c−0.4, 9.2, c+0.4] ⇒ **0.8 m opening through the 0.3 m slab**, one per room. Measured interior heights: 6 rooms 3.4–9.8 m, 12 rooms 1.8–4.2 m, **20 rooms 1.3–1.95 m** (so "20 rooms" is a stack of slots 1.3–1.95 m tall × 4.4–8.1 m wide, 8–12 px between 2-px slabs at 128).
- **Rasterisation** (`rasterize`, v293:72-85): cell-centre inclusion; `walls = ~(rooms ∪ corridor ∪ doors)` ⇒ GT "wall" = **all occupied**, i.e. slabs **and** the whole exterior margin (37–41% of the map). Slab thickness measured: **2 / 4 / 8 cells at 128/256/512**.
- **Poses** (`metric_trajectory`, v293:88-108): seed = world_id + `:poses:{agents}` ⇒ **N8 and N16 have different poses on the same world**; sizes share the same continuous poses. N/4 agents in the corridor, the rest in rooms (room = permutation with wrap-around, so N16 on 6 rooms puts 2 agents in 4 rooms). Three poses c−δ, c, c+δ with δ∈U(−0.1,0.1)² ⇒ **total motion ≤ 0.28 m** (sub-cell at 128, ≈7 cells at 512). "Real motion" is essentially absent.
- **Sensor** (`observe_trajectory`, v293:111-137 + `make_rays`/`raycast_observe`, v121:141-196): 256 fixed-angle Amanatides–Woo rays from the **snapped cell centre**, first-hit terminates the beam, free cells before the hit are written 0, the hit cell 1, nothing behind; then a strict 5 m disk mask around the continuous pose; `private = max` over the 3 poses. Consequence (verified, `probe_beams.py`): **observed wall cells are 100% surface cells adjacent to free** (2554/2554 at H256 r12 N16; GT has 29338 wall cells of which 2918 are surface). The supervision target for "wall" is therefore a 1-px first-hit line on the near face of a 2/4/8-px slab; the far face of the same slab is a different cell, observed only from the other room.
- **Observed fraction** (`probe_v293.py`, TRAIN idx 3): own-known 7.8–8.4% (6 rooms) and 4.5–4.6% (20 rooms) at 128, falling to 6.9–7.4% / 3.6% at 512; R8 union-known 51–69% (6 rooms N16) vs 20–24% (20 rooms N8). **Wall share of own-known: 128 7.5% (r6) – 12.9% (r20); 256 3.6–6.5%; 512 1.6–3.0%** — halves per doubling (1-px lines). Door cells in R8 union: 67–100% observed.
- **Beam gaps** (`probe_beams.py`, single pose in open space): angular spacing at 5 m = 0.1227 m = **0.79 / 1.57 / 3.14 cells** at 128/256/512. Open-space known fraction by range band: 128 = 100% everywhere; 256 = 99.1% (3–4 m), 93.8% (4–4.5 m), **86.1% (4.5–5 m)**; 512 = 99.5% (1–2 m), **81.2% (2–3 m), 58.5% (3–4 m), 48.0% (4–4.5 m), 43.1% (4.5–5 m)**. So at 512 the GT itself is a dotted line beyond ≈2 m, and the "wall" a decoder must reproduce is partly a sampling artefact of a 256-beam sensor, not geometry. (F3's "far-range dotted at 512" is correct; it also already applies at 256 beyond 4 m.)
- **Graph/rounds** (`select_forest`/`forest_targets`, bank:87-122): deterministic min-root BFS spanning forest over the 8 m radius graph; stage R targets = max over sources reached in R synchronous forest rounds. On the 48 VALIDATION 128 scenes (`probe_rounds.py`): physical diameters 2–5, 1/48 scenes disconnected; **R4 reached set == R8 reached set for 77.7% of recipients; 97.9% of recipients reach all N sources by R8.** Hence "R8" ≈ global union, and R4→R8 differences are re-sends of identical packets for most recipients.
- **Loss / decision** (for completeness): `proper_terms` = all-pixel known BCE mean + wall BCE summed over known cells / known count, per recipient (mapping_support_training_v306.py:31-35), stage weight 0.2 each in v306 (`STAGE_WEIGHT=.2`, line 12; F1's .5/.125 is the v299/v310 trainer, not checked here); ternary prediction `pred = where(known>=0, wall>=0, -1)` (train_mapping_multiscale_v310.py:444) — **fixed zero threshold on both logits**.

### 2.3 HouseExpo pilot (v121/v122) facts and what is missing

### 2.5 Specification of a leak-free HouseExpo + synthetic-furniture dataset (versioned, separate from the historical bank)

1. **Source & provenance**: pinned `json.tar.gz` (SHA dad4a7d2…, v121:30); record per house `source_json_sha256`, `normalized_contour_sha256`, `room_num`, bbox.
2. **House-level split, done once, before any rasterisation**: TRAIN/VAL/TEST = 80/10/10 by sha256(house id); TEST ids written to a sealed list and **never rasterised** in this phase. All H, N, trajectories, object draws, crops and augmentations of a house inherit its split.
3. **Near-duplicate rejection under D4**: rasterise every admitted contour at 0.2 m, canonicalise over the 8 rotations/reflections and translation, hash; additionally compute max IoU of each VAL/TEST house against all TRAIN houses after centroid alignment over the 8 symmetries and **drop VAL/TEST houses with IoU > 0.9** (SUNCG contains mirrored/re-used plans; exact-contour dedup is not enough). Log the IoU histogram.
4. **Charts (physical extent) and native resolution**: choose a fixed physical chart per house family — propose 20 m × 20 m to match v293 — and rasterise **directly from `verts` at H ∈ {128,256,512}** with the 4× supersample conservative rule (`rasterize_floor` generalised to any H), cell = 20/H = 0.156/0.078/0.039 m. Never upsample 128. Treat physical extent (e.g. 12.8 / 20 / 30 m) as a separate controlled axis.
5. **Sensor model must be declared per H**: either keep 256 beams and publish the gap curve of §2.1 as a property of the data, or set beams = ⌈2π·range/cell⌉ rounded up to a power of two (128→256, 256→512, 512→1024) so coverage is resolution-invariant. Mixing the two silently is a confound. Real motion: BFS cell paths with ≥ 3–5 m travelled per agent and several snapshots (v121 machinery), so occlusion by furniture and by walls actually occurs.
6. **Labels (evaluation-only rasters, never inputs)**: (a) `occupied_all` = ¬floor ∪ objects; (b) `wall_structural` = ¬floor cells within ≤ 0.35 m of the floor boundary **and** not exterior void (exterior void = ¬floor cells farther than 0.35 m from floor; counted in occupied_all but excluded from structural-wall F1 unless the sensor can hit them); (c) `object` = synthetic furniture cells (rect/circle/polygon/thin pole/narrow passage pairs) placed inside the floor polygon with a clearance rule, each with an instance id; (d) `door` = floor cells removed by a morphological opening of the floor with a 1.2 m disc that connect two different room components (or `room_category` adjacency). Observations remain ternary (unknown/free/occupied); label-conditioned metrics are "recovery within GT subregion", not semantic classification, exactly as README §5.3(6) demands.
7. **Per-scene manifest**: house id, split, H, extent, N, trajectory seed, object seed, beams, hashes of private/positions/adjacency/labels; refuse duplicates and cross-split geometry; bank is append-only with SHA-pinned manifests (reuse the v306 bank discipline).
8. **Cost (measured here, single CPU core, synthetic 20-vertex apartment, 16 agents, ≈4.2 m travel, 256 beams; `probe_cost.py`)**: rasterise + BFS component + trajectory/raycast = **0.33 s @128, 1.4 s @256, 5.9 s @512** (pure-Python BFS path planning dominates; raycast is vectorised). For 2,000 houses × 3 H × 2 N ≈ 8 CPU-hours single-thread, < 1 h on 16 cores. Storage: int8 private is 0.26/1.05/4.19 MB per 16-agent scene, but **np.savez_compressed is 4/7/19 KB** per scene (sparse ternary); packed 2-bit is 66/262/1049 KB. Write to `/mnt/datasets` (1.7 TB free), not `/home` (25.6 GB < 30 GB floor). The v306 bank itself regenerated 1776+144 scenes in 191 s (v306:304).
9. **Synthetic-furniture augmentation of the existing v293 family** (cheapest first step, same generator seeds, new version number): add per-room objects drawn from a seeded RNG, re-run the identical `observe_trajectory`, emit `object_mask`; keeps all historical comparability axes (H, rooms, N) while adding occlusion and non-wall occupied.

## (1) Hypotheses (this reviewer's, stated to be attacked)

- **D1 (target definition)**: the dominant source of low wall F1 in *every* family is that the target is a 1-px first-hit surface line (2.3% of own-known at 512 r6) scored pixel-exactly at a zero threshold; this interacts with resolution (surface line stays 1 px while the slab grows 2→8 px) and with the 256-beam sensor (GT becomes dotted beyond 2 m at 512).
- **D2 (plateau is multi-causal)**: the 60–63% R8 wall-F1 plateau at 128 across v299/v304/v310/v319 is jointly caused by (i) K0 recall loss (83→60 own after fusion), (ii) door sealing, (iii) VALIDATION selection — not by a single fusion-semantics defect.
- **D3 (new-family regression is not informative about capacity)**: v321–v340 results say nothing about 64-D limits; they are dominated by optimisation/initialisation failures (five K0=0 runs, cold-decoder surgery) and 10–19× slower updates in a fixed 7200 s lease.
- **D4 (compression target is mis-specified)**: on sparse ternary maps the strong lossless floor is ≈1–1.2 KB per agent map at 512 (19 KB per 16-agent scene), i.e. within ≈2× of one 576/608 B packet; "≥100× vs strong lossless" cannot be met by any 64×8-bit code on this data; only "vs uncompressed 2-bit raw" can.

## (3) Counter-evidence (against my own hypotheses and the root's)

- Against D1 being sufficient: v331/332 privileged fits with full freedom still only reach .85–.88 (128) / .67–.75 (256) under BCE+threshold — but these were 400 steps on 2 scenes; and v299's 4-px tolerance still leaves 41% of 20-room peer walls unmatched (missing segments, not misplacement). So a DT head fixes localisation, not missing structure.
- Against D2's "multi-causal": the v304 control has K0 .871 yet R8 .616 — the drop is after fusion, which supports H2 more than D1 for the *fusion* part. However v340's family shows the reverse (own F1 rises after fusion while FPR explodes), so "fusion erases own walls" is family-specific.
- Against D3: v335→v340 did show real but small monotone gains (K0 128 .504→.542→.579) under matched controls; the family is not purely broken, just far behind and slow.
- Against H1's remedy at 512: the GT at 512 is itself dotted (43% coverage at range); a DT to the nearest *observed* wall cell is not a distance to the wall; the head would learn the sensor's sampling pattern (A14). H1's "all sizes" claim is unsupported at 512.
- Against H2's remedy: q=0 public spatial ownership was tried (v306 support-local addresses) and collapsed; v329–v334 "ownership" variants all gave K0=0. The absence of positive evidence is not disproof (collapse ≠ capacity), but five failures with no positive control is a strong prior against spending the next 7200 s there.
- Against H3 as stated: the layout needs ≤ 80 numbers; with 255 levels per scalar and 20 m extent (7.8 cm) a 512-px-exact coordinate needs 9 bits > 8, so pixel-exact walls at 512 cannot be encoded one-scalar-per-coordinate; yet the binding term is the known-support boundary (≈42% of 262k cells with ragged beam edges at 512 r20 N16), which no 512-bit code can reproduce pixel-exactly — this makes union accuracy/FPR capped at 512, independent of architecture, and it is a property of the *sensor/metric design*, not only of 64 D.

## (4) Falsifiable experiments

Cheap CPU (no checkpoints needed; all < 10 min):

## (5) Verdicts

- **H1** — mechanism (pixel-BCE on 1-px lines + zero threshold ⇒ recall collapse, no distance-aware gradient): **supported, confidence 0.7** (P≫R in v340; tolerance curve in v299; surface-only targets measured). Remedy "DT head raises K0 and fused wall F1 substantially at all sizes": **unproven; 0.45 at 128/256 for ≥+3 pp K0, 0.25 at 512** (dotted GT; and misplacement explains at most ~half of missing walls at 20 rooms).
- **H2** — dense diagonal-Gaussian averaging cannot compose unions; needs public ownership / selection restrictions: the plateau and own-loss-after-fusion are **consistent** with H2 (0.5 that dense averaging is a binding limit at 128), but the proposed remedy has **zero positive evidence and six in-codebase failures** (v306, v329, v330, v333, v334, v337-type decoders); **0.3** that an ownership/selection design beats v310 within a 7200 s batch.
- **H3** — information cap at 512/20 rooms under 64×8 bits: **0.7 that pixel-exact union wall F1 and union accuracy/FPR are capped well below "high fidelity" at 512** — but the cap is dominated by the known-support boundary entropy and by the 256-beam dotted GT, not by the room layout (≤ 80 numbers). It is a data/metric statement first; redefine the 512 sensor and the tolerance before treating it as an architecture ceiling.
