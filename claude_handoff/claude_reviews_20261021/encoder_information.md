# Encoder / information-retention review — v310 (v299 global64) family

Role question (README §1): 64 个全局变量到底保留了哪些墙、物体、已知支持与几何细节？K0 漏墙是编码丢失、优化失败，还是 decoder 读不出？

Scope: read-only review of `handoff_src` (sealed v310 source, v32x–v34x scripts, configs, v319 endpoint ledger), plus local CPU runs of the v293 generator (statistics only, no model, no training, nothing under `handoff_src` modified). Weights are not available locally, so every statement about the trained code is either (i) taken from the saved VALIDATION ledgers, or (ii) framed as an experiment.

## 0. 中文摘要（先读结论）

1. **瓶颈机制（事实）**：K0 时 decoder 收到的不是 64×8 bit，而是 **64 个 hard8 均值（255 级，7.99 bit/维）+ 64 个连续 FP32 的 log 方差**（= −log q_learned，由同一 2048 特征经另一个 Linear 头产生）。K0 的 hard 码与 encoder 输出逐位相同（v319 审计 0/36864 坐标改变）。融合后 decoder 收到的是各到达源 hard 码的逐坐标精度加权平均再重量化，以及一个主要编码“来了几个源”的 log 方差。
2. **K0 不是信息预算问题**：按生成器几何量出的 K0 目标只需 7–12 条墙线坐标（≈50–110 bit 即可像素级放置，任何尺寸），远小于 512 bit。K0 漏墙只能是 encoder 没把信息放进码里 / decoder 画不出来 / 训练信号不够，三者之一或混合。
3. **K0 退化的实证规律（新发现，来自 v319 E0 = v310 E12 的逐层级 ledger）**：K0 预测墙像素数在 128/256/512 几乎恒定（86/99/106），而真墙像素 102/188/309；精度 0.91/0.83/0.63 基本保持，召回 0.78/0.45/0.27 按 ≈1/H 塌缩；known 头的像素数却随分辨率正确缩放（1005/3733/13694 vs 1002/3725/13594）。这与“模型对墙位置有一个**以米计近似恒定（≈7–10 cm）**的定位不确定性 + 像素 BCE 阈值”完全一致，而与“墙被整体遗忘”不一致。该不确定性在 encoder 还是 decoder 一侧，现有证据**不能**区分；本文设计的 E4/E5 可以。
4. **历史 GT-latent 证据只在 128、旧数据族上分离了 encoder 与 decoder**：v264（冻结 D，200 步只优化 64 hard8 码）own wall F1 82.99→96.27；v285/v286（冻结 D、固定协方差、只优化 64 均值）融合后 own wall F1 74.9→96.2；v265 的 “initial8” 显示仅用自身观测做 8 步局部码精修（**可部署，无特权**）就把 K0 wall F1 从 76.6 提到 86.1。即在 128 上，decoder 读得出、信息装得下、**是 amortized encoder 给不出那个码**。对 v310 在 256/512 和多房间数据上**没有任何同类证据**；v331/v332 是 v329 矩形渲染器的可达性，不是 v310 decoder 的。
5. **H3**：按 9 bit/坐标像素级放置，R8 union 的墙线坐标数在 512/rooms20/N16 ≈ 82 条 → ≈740 bit > 512 bit；rooms12/N16 ≈ 560 bit；rooms6 ≈ 290–300 bit 在预算内；128/rooms20/N16 ≈ 511 bit 正好在边界。因此 H3 对最大两个层级成立、对其余层级不成立，且差距是 1.1–1.5×，不是数量级。
6. **最先做什么**：在远端 GPU 上对冻结的 v310 E12 做一个不训练的诊断批（≤7200 s）：容差 F1 曲线、去量化解码、协方差通道消融、1 像素平移码碰撞测试、经冻结 decoder 的特权 64 码拟合（K0 与 R8）、以及可部署的 8 步测试时码精修。它们直接回答本角色的问题，并且第 6 项可能不训练就改善 v310。

## 1. Hypotheses

Root hypotheses H1/H2/H3 are addressed in §5. My own, encoder-side hypotheses (each falsified by a named experiment in §4):

- **H-E1 (amortization gap, not representational loss, at 128)**: the 64+64 input space of the frozen v310 decoder contains codes that reproduce K0 maps far better than the encoder's output; the K0 deficit at 128 is the amortized encoder failing to find them. Prediction: E5 warm-start fit gains ≥ +10 pp K0 wall F1 at 128; E6 (8 deployable local steps) gains ≥ +3 pp.
- **H-E2 (fixed physical localization uncertainty)**: the model localizes walls to ≈ ±7–10 cm independent of H; pixel-BCE + threshold 0 turns this into recall ≈ 1/(2k_px) → 0.78/0.45/0.27. Prediction: tolerance-F1 (E1) at ±1 px (256) and ±2 px (512) recovers most of the 128 level; predicted-wall counts become resolution-independent (already observed, §2.3).
- **H-E3 (where the uncertainty lives is unknown; three sub-cases)**: (a) encoder/quantization: the hard8 code does not change under 1-px map shifts at 512 (E4), or unquantized decode helps (E2); (b) decoder rendering: the 16×16 lift grid (1.25 m cells at all H) + shared conv weights cannot sharpen to 1 px at 512 even for the best code (E5 fails from all starts); (c) loss: wall term weight per wall pixel shrinks ∝ 1/H (wall fraction of known cells 8–14 % at 128, 4–7 % at 256, 1.8–3.3 % at 512, measured §2.4), so the optimizer under-invests in walls at high H (CPU surrogate S3).
- **H-E4 (precision channel is content)**: at K0 the 64 continuous log-variances carry map content that the decoder uses; neutralizing them (E3) costs several pp of K0 wall F1, and after fusion this channel is scrambled by summation (contributing to the K0→R1 own-wall drop 0.83→0.67 at 128).
- **H-E5 (K0 is never budget-limited; R8 is budget-limited only in the largest strata)**: see §2.5.

## 2. Evidence (file:line citations)

### 2.1 (a) Exact bottleneck mechanics

Quantizer (not 127 levels — correction to brief F1):
- `reference_source_v310/scripts/mapping_implicit_model_v192.py:37-39` `latent_codes`: `round(clamp(z)·127)+127` → integers 0..254 = **255 levels**, 127 = exact zero, 255 reserved; `:42-46` decode `(c−127)/127`; `:55-60` `latent_quantize` = hard forward + identity STE inside [−1,1] (zero gradient only where the clamp binds; tanh never leaves [−1,1], so the STE is always identity). Step = 2/254 = 0.00787 of full scale = 7.87 cm of the 20 m extent.
- Encoder: `mapping_local_capacity_v269.py:68-73` five `Conv2d(·,·,3,stride=2,padding=1)+SiLU` with widths 32/64/96/128/128 → 128×4×4 = 2048 → `global_head Linear(2048,64)`; `:138-141` `encode` = `latent_quantize(tanh(global_head(features)))`. Input channels `:135-136`: `[known, free, wall, public_x, public_y]`, x/y = cell-centre grid of `coordinate_grid` (`v192:26-34`).
- Precision head: `mapping_recognition_hops_v277.py:30-31` `PRECISION_MIN,PRECISION_MAX = 1e-4, 4.`, `LATENT_PRIOR = 1e-4`; `:54-57` `Linear(2048,64)` initialized with zero weight and a bias giving q = 1; `:104-118` `q = active·(1e-4 + 3.9999·sigmoid(head))`, `precision = q + 1e-4/(N·64)`, `h = q·μ` (FP64 assembly). The prior is ≤ 1.95e-7 (N=8) — numerically irrelevant (observed q_min over all VALIDATION scenes is 2.4e-3…6.2e-3, never near the 1e-4 floor: `configs/mapping_restriction_k0_audit_v319_epoch1.json`, field `precision_min`).
- Native adapter: `mapping_multiscale_v299_v310.py:45-58` builds the same five channels at native H; `:79-92` runs the *same* conv weights on the native raster → 128×(H/32)² → `avg_pool2d(kernel=stride=H/128)` → 2048 → same `global_head`/`precision_head`. So 128 is un-pooled; 256 averages 2×2; 512 averages 4×4 final feature vectors before one shared linear head.
- What the decoder receives, at every round including K0: `mapping_multiroom_forest_v295.py:160-165` `scalar_belief` = (h + Σ msg η)/(q + Σ msg p), variance = reciprocal; `:319-320` `code = latent_quantize(mean)`, `feature = belief_features(code, diag(variance))`; `mapping_belief_readout_v282.py:48-62` → **[64 hard8 mean | 64 log marginal variance (continuous FP32)]**. At K0 the mean is q μ/(q+ε), re-hardened → the encoder's own hard code (the v319 K0 audit measures `raw_mean_minus_mu_max ≈ 3e-5`, i.e. prior shrinkage ≤ 8e-5 relative, which cannot move an on-grid value across a 1/127 step; the same audit's `mean_code_coordinates_changed = 0/36864` per size shows the restriction edit also left the mean code untouched), and log-variance = −log(q_learned+ε) ∈ [−1.39, 9.2] — **unquantized, computed locally from the precision head**. So the K0 decoder input is 64×7.99 bits + 64 FP32 numbers, i.e. a second 64-dimensional linear readout of the same 2048 features. "64×8 = 512 bits" (brief F7) is the *wire* budget for the means only; on the wire the precisions are also sent (profile 4: 255-level log grid on [1e-8,128], `v295:172-194`; packet 172 B), so the declared per-message budget is 128×8 = 1024 bits, of which only the 64 means survive fusion as content (precisions are summed).
- Decoder path: `mapping_common_map_v284.py:127-147` (`CommonMap64.decode`): `extra = (logvar − TRAIN mean)/TRAIN std`; `lift Linear(128→2048)` (first 64 columns = original lift, extra 64 columns zero-initialized at v282 `:196-197`, trained since v284); split 1024 `mean_raw` → tanh, 1024 `q_raw` → `Q_MIN+(Q_MAX−Q_MIN)·sigmoid` with `Q_MIN,Q_MAX = 1e-4, 4.` (`v269:20`); `h = q·tanh(mean_raw)`; reshaped to 4 planes × 16×16 (`ContextCodecConfig side=16, planes=4`, `v269:41-46`); `ContextDecoder` (`mapping_context_codec_v121.py:149-183`): inputs `mean = h/q`, `precision = log1p(q)` (`:171-172`), plus 4 public channels `[yy, xx, 1/H, 1/W]` from `occupancy_codec/global_spatial_source.py:48-53` (endpoint-inclusive `linspace(−1,1,H)` chart — note the encoder's x/y channel is the cell-centre chart; the two differ by ≤ ½ px at the borders at every H, a fixed learnable offset, not an explanation of anything); lift conv 3×3 (12→128→128); four blocks (96,64,48,32) of two 3×3 convs each at feature extents 16/32/64/128 (`:175-178`), or `H/8,H/4,H/2,H` at native (`v310:40-42,123-126`) with bilinear interpolation of *features*; `output Conv2d(32,2,1)` → known logit, wall|known logit. `v288:89-99` runs this decode twice with identical features in the `joint_belief` arm (no information difference). Parameter counts (constructed locally): encoder convs 333,664; global head 131,136; precision head 131,136; lift 264,192; decoder 527,842.
- Loss `mapping_support_training_v306.py:16-39`: `known_per = mean over all H² pixels of BCE(known)`, `wall_per = Σ_{known} BCE(wall)/n_known`; stage weights `train_mapping_multiscale_v310.py:44` `{0:.5, 1:.125, 2:.125, 4:.125, 8:.125}`. Metric `mapping_recognition_metrics_v277.py:36-46`: wall F1 over target-known cells, `pred==1` only (a cell predicted *unknown* is a wall false negative); `:85` unknown FPR = predicted known / target-unknown.
- v329→v340 encoder for contrast (`scripts/mapping_geometric_ownership_v329.py:47-89`): `PixelUnshuffle(2)` → conv 32 ch at H/2 (RF 6 px), coarse branch two stride-2 convs → 32 ch at H/8 (RF 18 px), 64 learned queries attend (softmax over all H/2 and H/8 positions, keys = content + public `[x,y,x²,y²,level]`) → 64 tokens × 32 → `CoordinateHead` (`:37-44`) collapses each token to one scalar μ_k and one q_k = EPS+softplus (`:193-202`, EPS=1e-4 from `mapping_local_extrinsic_v324.py:23`). **No 8-bit quantization anywhere; the v324 wire is FP32** (`v324:89-92,180`). Its K0 wall F1 is 0.554/0.422 at 128/256 (`configs/mapping_endpoint_v340_fp_representation.json`, 主张一) — i.e. removing quantization and doubling the wire did not help K0; the encoder/decoder design dominates bit depth.

### 2.2 (b) Receptive field, pooling, survival of 1-px lines

Computed locally with the exact conv stack (random weights; RF is weight-independent):

| H | final grid | RF of one final cell | stride | RF of one pooled 4×4 cell | cell of 16×16 decoder lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 128 | 4×4 | 63 px = 9.84 m | 32 px = 5.0 m | 63 px = 9.84 m | 8 px = 1.25 m |
| 256 | 8×8 | 63 px = 4.92 m | 32 px = 2.5 m | 95 px = 7.42 m | 16 px = 1.25 m |
| 512 | 16×16 | 63 px = 2.46 m | 32 px = 1.25 m | 159 px = 6.21 m | 32 px = 1.25 m |

- The stack is not structurally blind to 1-px lines: each stride-2 3×3/pad-1 layer covers every input pixel (even columns through the centre tap, odd columns through the side taps of two neighbours), so a 1-px wall produces nonzero activations at all depths; sub-stride position is representable only through which tap path fired, i.e. through learned nonlinear gain.
- Measured (random init, pooled 2048 features): L2 change per 1-px shift of a vertical 1-px wall = 1.5 % / 0.8 % / 0.3 % of the feature norm at 128/256/512. After the shared `Linear(2048→64)`+tanh, a 0.3 % feature change must be amplified above the hard8 step (0.79 % of full scale) to register at all. Whether the trained head does so is exactly experiment E4; the architecture neither guarantees nor forbids it.
- Physical scale conflict: the same 3×3 filters span 47 cm at 128 and 12 cm at 512; the same linear head consumes un-pooled 9.8 m-RF features at 128 and 4×4-averaged 2.5 m-RF features at 512. The v310 continuation fine-tuned all three jointly (one set of weights). The decoder's lift grid is 1.25 m per cell at every H, and its first block runs on a 2×/4× bilinear blow-up of that grid at 256/512.

Readings relevant to the role question:

Precision stays high-ish, recall collapses ≈ ∝ 1/H (ratios 0.57, 0.61). Under a simple misplacement model (1-px lines placed with error uniform in ±k), recall = 1/(2k_px) gives k ≈ 0.64/1.1/1.8 px = **10/8.7/7.2 cm** — a roughly constant physical localization uncertainty. 3. - **K0 is never budget-limited**: ≤ 110 bits for pixel-exact walls at 512, ≲ 200 with the pose; the deployed input even has 64 extra continuous numbers. K0 failure is therefore encoder-amortization, decoder rendering, training signal, or target dotting — not capacity.
- **R8**: wall lines alone exceed 512 bits for rooms20/N16 at every H ≥ 256 (and sit at 511 at 128), and for rooms12/N16 at 512; rooms6 strata fit at all H. Pixel-exact *known support* for N16 (three distinct poses per agent, dotted far range at 512) needs 864 bits — impossible under 512 means; one-pose approximations (288) fit. A 1-px-tolerant metric saves one bit per coordinate (rooms20/N16 at 512: 656, still over). Exploiting generator priors (fixed corridor x, near-fixed room near-edges) cannot reduce the 20-room world below 3·rooms−2 = 58 free numbers ≈ 522 bits at 9 bits. So H3 is right for the two largest strata by a factor 1.1–1.5, not by an order of magnitude, and wrong for the four smaller strata; the equal-stratum macro is capped below 1 but far above today's 0.43.
- Caveat on the "ideal code": these are counts for a parametric geometry code the decoder can render exactly. The deployed dense tanh code is not such a code; the budget says only that the *ceiling* is not the explanation for K0 and is only partially the explanation at R8/512.

### 2.6 (d) Does the historical GT-latent / attainability evidence separate encoder loss from decoder unreadability?

Yes, but only at native 128, on the older rectangle data family, and for ancestors of the v310 decoder:
- `configs/mapping_direct_code_closure_v264.json` ("single_question": frozen 32k decoder, 200 Adam code-only steps, hard8 forward, own-observed BCE): VALIDATION medium own-A rectangle **wall F1 82.99 → 96.27**, interior 73.33 → 94.51; TRAIN 82.91 → 93.93. Same decoder, same 64-byte code space, better code exists → at 128 the K0 deficit was the amortized encoder, not the decoder and not the bits.
- `configs/mapping_posttraining_capacity_v285.json` + `mapping_fixed_c_capacity_interpretation_v286.json` (frozen v284 equality checkpoint, covariance held bitwise fixed, 200 steps on the 64 means from the analytic common mean, selected by proper BCE): VALIDATION own wall F1 74.9 → 96.2 (base) / 75.0 → 90.9 (medium), peer 76.0 → 96.3 / 67.6 → 87.1, union acc 92.9 → 98.5 / 90.3 → 96.5, FPR 3.18 → 1.08 / 3.60 → 2.52. Fused case: the decoder can read a fused code with ~96 % walls; GBP averaging of recognition codes does not produce it (consistent with H2).
- `configs/mapping_semiamortized_encoder_v265.json` "initial8_diagnostic": **8 local Adam steps on the code using only the agent's own observation** (deployable: no labels, no peer data, bottleneck unchanged) raised medium-rectangle K0 wall F1 76.62 → 86.19, interior 67.94 → 81.02, topology 76.8 → 85.2. The *trained* semi-amortized variant failed to beat "control + 8 steps", and the branch was closed — but the +9.6 pp from test-time refinement itself was never carried into v29x/v31x (`RecognitionHops64.factors` is a single forward, `v277:90-118`).
- Negative history that matters for any "just train the encoder to the fitted codes" plan: `mapping_code_distillation_execution_v233.json` (teacher = refined codes; both arms selected step 0), v251 K8 code anchor failed, v250 failed pre-update. Fitted codes are per-scene nonconvex solutions and did not transfer as regression targets.
- `configs/mapping_attainability_v331_v332_synthesis.json` is **not** evidence about v310: it fits 576 rectangle-renderer scalars of the v329 geometry decoder (wall F1 0.85–0.88 at 128, 0.67–0.75 at 256, "thin/fragmented walls still missing, especially 256"). It shows that *that* decoder family is itself the limit at 256; it says nothing about the v310 CNN decoder or about 512.
- Nothing exists for v310 at 256/512 or on the v293 multi-room bank. That gap is what §4 fills.

## 3. Counter-evidence (against my own hypotheses and against the root's framing)

- Against "quantization is the K0 limiter": the v321–v340 family uses FP32 means/precisions on the wire and no hard8, yet its K0 wall F1 (0.554 at 128, 0.422 at 256) is far below v310 (0.831/0.551). Bit depth is not what separates these models; the 64-scalar attention pooling + implicit/periodic decoders are.
- Against "the encoder is pixel-blind by construction": the conv stack covers every pixel; sensitivity to 1-px shifts is small but nonzero at 512 and is a matter of trained gain (E4 decides).
- Against H-E1 generalizing to v310: the v264/v285 witnesses used older decoders (mean-only or v284), simpler rectangle worlds, 128 only, and privileged labels; v233/v251 show the fitted codes did not become learnable targets. A positive privileged fit on v310 would prove decoder reachability, not that an encoder can learn to emit it.
- Against the constant-count/misplacement reading (§2.3): precision also falls with H (0.91→0.63), so part of the deficit is genuine misprediction, not only suppression; and ≈20 % of 512 wall cells sit in dotted, low-known-density zones where the *target* itself is sub-threshold under any smooth predictor.
- Against H-E3c (loss scaling) as a fix: re-weighting walls only moves the BCE-optimal threshold; with localization uncertainty k px, a thick line of width 2k has F1 = 2/(2k+1) (0.67 at k=1, 0.40 at k=2). Re-weighting trades precision for recall; only reducing k raises F1.
- Against H3 as stated ("regardless of architecture"): the budget is exceeded by 1.1–1.5× in two strata only, and the known-support term dominates it; an architecture with strong priors and a tolerant metric moves the boundary. H3 is a stratum statement, not a global cap.
- Against reading the ledger as "v310 K0": the table is the VALIDATION development panel (used for selection repeatedly, README §5.1), equal-stratum means; threshold 0, no post-processing.

### 4.2 One 7200 s GPU batch (frozen v310 E12 `u000021312-e0013…pt`, loaded via `MultiscaleV299.from_payload(payload['model'])`, zero weight updates, matched controls; sizes 128/256/512; all 144 VALIDATION scenes + 24 fixed TRAIN scenes (worlds 0–7 × rooms 6/12/20, N8); `PYTHONPATH=.:exploration_grid` from the sealed source; outputs to a new diagnostic directory)

- **E0 parity**: reproduce the ledger's epoch-0 K0/R1/R8 metrics (gate: |Δ| ≤ 1e-3 on every mean in §2.3; otherwise stop — the harness is wrong).

## 5. Verdict on H1 / H2 / H3

- **H1 (precision; pixel-BCE + smooth decoder → recall collapse; DT head fixes it)**. Mechanism: **supported, confidence 0.8** — the ledger shows precision maintained, recall ∝ 1/H, resolution-independent wall pixel counts, correctly scaling known counts; the implied localization uncertainty is ≈ 7–10 cm at all H. Fix as stated: **confidence 0.35**. A DT regression with `wall := DT < 0.5` inherits the same threshold problem (with uncertainty ±k px the expected DT minimum is ≈ k/2 px, > 0.5 px at 256/512, so it also predicts no wall); what DT buys is a distance-aware gradient that can *reduce* k during training. Whether k is reducible by the decoder/loss at all depends on whether the code carries the position (E4/E5). Use a ridge/argmin decision rule, not an absolute DT threshold, if H1 is tried.
- **H2 (fusion semantics; dense averaging cannot compose unions)**. From the encoder side: **supported, confidence 0.7** for the diagnosis — one exchange costs 16 pp of own-wall F1 at 128 while the own hard code is merely averaged with one neighbour and the log-variance shifts; the v285 witness shows a readable fused code exists that precision-weighted averaging does not produce. The prescribed remedy (public spatial ownership / selection restrictions) is a design claim I did not evaluate; note that any union-carrying code must stay within ≈512 content bits (§2.5), so "ownership" must be spatial *and* compact.
- **H3 (512 + 20 rooms information-theoretically capped)**. **Partly supported, confidence 0.6 for rooms20/N16 and rooms12/N16 at 512 (and rooms20/N16 at 128 sits at the edge); not supported for rooms6 strata at any H, nor for K0 at any H.** The cap is 1.1–1.5× over budget for wall lines and larger for pixel-exact dotted supports; it is a stratum-level ceiling below 1, not a reason for today's 0.43.
- Role question answer: 64 variables retain the coarse known support at all H (94→86 % known recall) and most wall pixels at 128; they lose 0.8 m doors (44 % door-free recall at 128 K0) and lose wall pixels in proportion to H. **K0 漏墙不是信息预算损失**（≤110 bit 就够）；在 128 上历史证据指向 **amortized encoder 优化不足**（decoder 能读到 96 % 的码，8 步局部精修可部署地 +9.6 pp）；在 256/512 上 encoder-side（1 像素不敏感/量化）与 decoder-side（1.25 m lift 网格 + 共享卷积画不出 1 px）**目前无法区分**，E4/E5 是区分它们的最便宜实验。

## 6. What I would do FIRST and why

Run the frozen-v310 diagnostic batch (§4.2) before any training, with E6 (deployable 8-step local code refinement) as its payload. Reasons: (1) it is the only experiment family that has historically produced a clean separation (v264/v285) and it has never been run on v310 or at 256/512; (2) it needs no new weights, no new data, no protocol relaxation, fits one 7200 s lease with a 1.25× margin, and every branch of its gates changes the next decision (encoder-limited → change the encoder/head or add local refinement; decoder-limited → the H1 decoder work; pixel-blind at 512 → stop promising pixel-exact 512 under 8-bit means); (3) E6 may improve the deployed anchor's K0 without training, which is the cheapest possible test of "optimization failure vs loss". In parallel, the CPU surrogate (§4.1 C3) validates the fit/gate methodology so the GPU batch is not the first time it runs. Do not start the H1 DT-head training until E1/E5 show the walls are present-but-misplaced rather than absent.
