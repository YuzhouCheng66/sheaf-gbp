### 1.2 The anchor comparison: what CAN and CANNOT be claimed

CAN: (a) on the identical 48 VAL scenes, identical sealed metric, identical 6-stratum equal-weight macro (protocol `evaluation.panel`; v318 `macro`; v342 L404-411; v310 `metric_table` L195-215 — verified equivalent, since within a stratum all scenes have the same N so recipient-mean and scene-mean coincide) and identical 172 B/send budget, the v342 owned final weights exceed the recorded v310 E12 numbers on K0 wall F1 (+7.9 pp), R8 union wall F1 (+22.2 pp), R8 accuracy (+5.0 pp) and R8 FPR (-2.1 pp); (b) the same holds on FRESH for v342, where no anchor number exists; (c) the anchor's number was itself VAL-selected (max R8 peer F1 over the v310 curve, v318 `primary_scope`), which biases the *anchor* upward, so the gap is conservative on that axis; (d) inside the matched pair, owned semantics causes +14.1 pp R8 over dense (VAL and FRESH), pending the paired CI of section 2.

## 2. Paired per-world statistic (to be computed on the remote host)

Files (both arms, under `/mnt/datasets/yuzhou-sheaf-gbp-research/claude-diag-20261021/runs/v342b-pair-long/{owned,dense}/`): `validation/final-val.json` (written L470-471) and `validation/final-fresh.json` (L475-476). Statistic: per scene `Δ_s = owned.hops['8'].wall_f1 - dense.hops['8'].wall_f1`; point estimate `Δ̄ = mean over the 6 (rooms,N) strata of the 8-scene mean of Δ_s` (linear, so it equals the difference of the two reported `all` numbers, +0.141 on VAL). Resampling unit = **world** `(rooms, index)`: the N8 and N16 scenes of one world share geometry (only poses differ) and must stay together — stratified cluster bootstrap (Field & Welsh 2007): inside each rooms stratum draw 8 world indices with replacement, keep both N scenes of each drawn world, recompute `Δ̄`; B = 10 000; percentile 95 % CI. Secondary: sign count over the 24 world means (>= 21/24 is two-sided binomial p = 2.8e-4), per-stratum sign, and the scene-level SD of Δ_s (to show the paired SD is far below the unpaired one; the two arms share trunk init and data order, which is the "same base model" case in which Berg-Kirkpatrick et al. 2012 observe significance at smaller gains).

Acceptance thresholds (apply to VAL **and** FRESH separately; both must hold):
- **G2**: CI lower bound of `Δ̄(R8 wall_f1) >= +0.05`; >= 21/24 worlds positive; all 6 strata positive. CI lower bound in [0, 0.05) = "significant but below the registered margin"; < 0 = fail.

- **G1 (within owned)**: `Δ_s = own_wall_f1[R8] - own_wall_f1[R0]` per scene; the gate already fails at the point (-0.073); the CI upper bound `< -0.02` confirms the failure is not noise. Report the dense arm's value the same way (-0.137).

If the v310 run's `validation/epoch-0012.json` (remote, 144 per-scene records with `hops[str(k)]`) is readable, do the per-scene paired version on the same 48 identities; otherwise the comparison stays point-vs-point and must be labelled so.
- Also report (no gate): `Δ̄` and CI for K0 `wall_f1`, R1 `wall_f1`, R8 `own_wall_f1`, R8 `union_accuracy`.

Expected magnitude: with a per-world SD of Δ_s of 0.03-0.08, the CI half-width is 1.5-3.5 pp, so G2 (+14.1) and G3 (-2.2 vs +0.5 margin) should pass comfortably; the computation is still required because 24 worlds is the whole evidence base and the per-stratum sign condition is the one that can fail (rooms20/N16 is where the dense arm and the anchor degrade least uniformly).

- **D2 own-retention split** (explains the G1 failure): `own_wall_f1@R8` on own blocks that no reached peer observed (posterior == own up to prior and wire quantisation) vs own blocks shared with a reached peer; reached sets from `bank.forest_targets`, block support from `own >= 0`. If the drop sits in shared blocks, the dilution is the averaging of two different scalars for one partially-observed block (protocol diagnostic 4); if it sits in own-only blocks too, it is the decoder's cross-block context.

## 4. Primary sources consulted and how they bear on this evaluation

- Martin, Fowlkes, Malik, "Learning to detect natural image boundaries using local brightness, color, and texture cues", IEEE TPAMI 26(5), 2004 ([pdf](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Research/Projects/CS/vision/grouping/papers/mfm-pami-boundary.pdf)): boundary precision/recall with a fixed localisation tolerance (1 % of the image diagonal, about 2.88 px), one-to-one minimum-cost bipartite matching so duplicate detections count as false positives, max F-measure as summary. The sealed `wall_f1` is the tolerance-0 limit of this family (F4); D1 above is the standard way to report sensitivity to misplacement.
- Arbelaez, Maire, Fowlkes, Malik, "Contour detection and hierarchical image segmentation", IEEE TPAMI 33(5), 2011 ([pdf](https://vision.ics.uci.edu/papers/ArbelaezMFM_PAMI_2011/ArbelaezMFM_PAMI_2011.pdf)): ODS = best F at one threshold fixed for the whole dataset; OIS = best threshold per image; AP = area under PR. The fixed DT<0.5 rule is an ODS-type (dataset-fixed, here pre-registered) operating point; OIS-style per-scene thresholds must never be used; AP/AUPRC is the threshold-free number A3 asked for.
- Csurka, Larlus, Perronnin, "What is a good evaluation measure for semantic segmentation?", BMVC 2013 ([pdf](https://bmva-archive.org.uk/bmvc/2013/Papers/paper0032/paper0032.pdf)): BF score = F1 of boundary points within a distance θ (0.75 % of the diagonal), motivated by region IoU not measuring contour accuracy; same message as D1.
- Shit et al., "clDice - a novel topology-preserving loss function for tubular structure segmentation", CVPR 2021 ([arXiv 2003.07311](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.07311v6)): voxel-wise overlap averages do not weight thin structures equally and a single-voxel shift can change the topology of the whole network; hence skeleton-based topology precision/sensitivity. The project's `pair_topology_accuracy` / `spurious_pair_connection_rate` on free cells is the analogous connectivity metric and belongs in the headline table (section 3).
- Birk & Carpin, "Merging occupancy grid maps from multiple robots", Proc. IEEE 94(7), 2006 ([pdf](https://robotics.ucmerced.edu/sites/robotics.ucmerced.edu/files/page/documents/pro2006.pdf)): map agreement is counted only over cells known in both maps (agr/dis; unknown cells affect neither), acceptance index 1 - agr/(agr+dis). The project's `union_accuracy` is this agreement rate made stricter (an unknown prediction on a target-known cell counts as wrong, metrics L18), and section 1 A1 shows its floor is .887 here; it cannot replace wall/topology metrics.
- Berg-Kirkpatrick, Burkett, Klein, "An empirical investigation of statistical significance in NLP", EMNLP 2012 ([pdf](https://nlp.cs.berkeley.edu/pubs/BergKirkpatrick-Burkett-Klein_2012_Significance_paper.pdf)): paired bootstrap over test items (b = 10^6 in their runs), test-set size is the main determinant of discrimination, and systems from the same base model are highly correlated and reach significance at smaller gains — exactly the matched-pair situation here.
- Field & Welsh, "Bootstrapping clustered data", JRSS-B 69(3), 2007 ([record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jorssb/v69y2007i3p369-390.html)): resample clusters, not observations, when observations within a cluster are correlated — the reason the unit in section 2 is the world, not the scene or the recipient.
