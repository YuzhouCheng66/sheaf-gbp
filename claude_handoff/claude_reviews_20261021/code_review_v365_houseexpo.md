## Findings

### (a) Split integrity: PASS
1. **NOTE: the split is assigned from the contour hash before any rasterisation or pose sampling** (L62-64). `build_index` hashes the raw `verts` with v121 `geometry_hash`, which is invariant to translation, cyclic order and reversal. The split is `sha256('v365:split:'+hash) % 10`, so exact duplicates must share a split. Dedup comes after that (L68-71) and keeps the lowest id. On the actual index: 22,915 houses (TRAIN 20,366 / VALIDATION 2,549), 0 duplicate ids, 0 duplicate hashes, 0 hashes in more than one split, no TEST rows (`test_houses_withheld` = 2,502).
2. **NOTE: the index is complete and correct.** My independent re-scan found 23,240 eligible non-TEST files. The 325 not in the index are all exact-duplicate non-representatives. There are 0 split mismatches, 0 extra ids, and 371 exact-duplicate files in total. `world_spec` re-hashes the json on every load and refuses a mismatch (L103-104). The translated verts still give the index hash (checked).
3. **NOTE: TEST cannot be generated.** `world_spec` and `scene_plan` raise on anything other than TRAIN/VALIDATION (L97-98, L202-203), and TEST houses are not in `lists`. The TEST json files are still physically present in the json dir (and on the 5090 path), so the seal depends on the index, not on the filesystem.
4. **NOTE: no rotated or mirrored copies leak between TRAIN and VALIDATION.** The D4-invariant exact hash found 0 TRAIN/VALIDATION groups. At 0.1 m rounding (near duplicates with different vertex counts allowed) there are 5 VALIDATION houses with a TRAIN twin. None is in the used VALIDATION panels: their VALIDATION list indices are 1071, 91, 199, 492 and 499, all ≥ 16. One TRAIN twin (stratum 3, index 176) is in the 600 TRAIN houses actually used, but its VALIDATION partner is index 499, which is never evaluated. So this launch has no leakage. For later use of VALIDATION beyond index 15, or TEST, a raster-IoU near-duplicate pass is still undone; the docstring (L12-13) states the limitation honestly.
5. **NOTE: the panels are disjoint.** VALIDATION panel indices 0-7 and the trainer's fresh panel (index+8 → 8-15) use 24 and 24 distinct houses with no overlap (each house is used at both N8 and N16, as in v293). Neither panel shares a house with the 30 TRAIN houses tested. All lists are long enough: TRAIN needs ≤ 200 per stratum (smallest stratum has 5,383); VALIDATION needs 16 (smallest has 664).

### (b) Fixture contract and sensor parity: PASS
6. **NOTE: all 141 generated fixtures pass every contract check, with 0 failures** (30 TRAIN, 48 VALIDATION, 48 fresh at H128; 12 at H256; 3 at H512). The checks are listed under Method. Timing per fixture: mean 0.5 s (max 0.8 s) at H128, about 1.3 s at H512. The 600+48+48 scenes for the planned run take about 5 min of CPU.
7. **NOTE: the sensor and radio are exactly v293's.** `make_fixture` calls `d293.observe_trajectory` itself (L187) and v121 `radius_adjacency` with `d293.RADIO_RADIUS` (L188), which is the function v293 imports. `public_frame` is bit-equal to `bank.expected_frame(H)`. The walls dtype is int8 instead of v293's uint8. It is accepted by `observe_trajectory` (the `isin(0,1)` check), and the observations matched the walls on every fixture.
8. **NOTE: nothing leaks into the model inputs.** `inputs` holds only private/positions/trajectory/adjacency/public_frame. Geometry (`walls`, `geometry_spec` with `verts_m=None`) lives only in `evaluation`, and the trainer reads only `evaluation['door_mask']`, for metrics (train L472). The pose sampler is geometry-assisted, as in v293. A public geometric prior that v293 does not have: every house is bbox-centred in the chart (L105-106).
9. **MINOR: the manifest is thinner than v293's and `checked_arrays` cannot be applied** (L194-196). There is no `geometry_sha256`, `adjacency_sha256`, `source_sha256`, `walls_sha256` or graph block, and the evaluation has extra keys. `bank.checked_arrays` itself is v293-bound (`data.world_spec`, `evaluation == {'door_mask'}`). The trainer never calls it, so this does not block the launch, but per-scene provenance is weaker. The run's `run.json` does not record the index or data-module SHA either. `chain_day3_H.sh` does write both into `runs/v367-hx-train/lease.json`, which is sufficient for this batch.
10. **MINOR: there is no argument validation in `world_spec`** (L95-100). `lst[int(index)]` accepts negative indices, which silently wrap to the end of the list, and bools. The trainer only passes 0-15 and 0-199, so the launch is unaffected.

### (c) Pose sampling: PASS
11. **NOTE: the pose sampler is deterministic and correct.** The seed comes from `world_id`+N (L154). The largest component uses `ndimage.label`'s default cross structure, which is 4-connectivity (L156-160). Clearance is `distance_transform_edt` on the conservative 512 raster, ≥ 0.45 m (L162-163). The margin argument: a pose is at most 0.141 m from its centre (|δ| ≤ 0.1 per axis), and a coarse cell's farthest point is at most 0.221 m from the pose at H128. That adds up to ≤ 0.36 m < 0.45 m, so the containing cell lies inside the contour at every coarser conservative raster. Empirically, on every fixture all three poses of every agent were free at 128, 256 and 512.
12. **MINOR (interpretation, not a bug): the pose and graph distribution differ from v293, so "only the world geometry changes" is approximate.** Houses are small (free fraction about 0.22-0.25 of the 20 m chart, versus 0.61 for v293), and centres are uniform over the free area rather than spread across rooms and corridor. As a result:
    - Radio edge density is 0.81-0.85 versus 0.45 in v293.
    - 11 of 48 VALIDATION scenes (and 11 of 48 fresh) are complete graphs, where R1 = R2 = R4 = R8.
    - The 1.0 m separation is relaxed to 0.5 m in 13-14 of 48 scenes; 1 VALIDATION scene ends below 0.5 m.
    - R8 known fraction: 0.246 VALIDATION versus 0.532 in v293. Own known fraction: 0.079 versus 0.061.

    Read the expectation "in-domain R8 within 5 pp of v293 .924/.938" with this in mind. Multi-hop differences will be compressed on this family.

### (d) Opening mask: PASS with caveat
13. **NOTE: the opening mask matches its documentation** (L125-148). A cell is marked when it lies in a free run bounded by occupied cells at both ends (not by the image border), 0.4-1.5 m long, and its component spans ≤ 0.6 m across the run. The mask is bool and a subset of free cells by construction. It overlaps an occupied cell in 0 of 120 houses at 128 and 512, and in 0 of 141 fixtures. Openings with no labelled cells: 7/120 houses (5.8 %) at H128 and 4/120 (3.3 %) at H512. In the H128 VALIDATION panel 3 of 24 houses have none, and the fresh panel has 0. These scenes give `door_known_cells = 0`, which `node_metrics` handles. Openings are about 1.1 % of free cells. The `& (private != 1).all(0)` term (L193) is a no-op, because openings are free and observations match the walls. The semantics are resolution-dependent: thin openings close at 128 because of the conservative raster. Report this metric as "opening free recall", as stated in L15-17.
